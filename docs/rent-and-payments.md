# Rent and payments

## When rent is due
Rent is due on the 1st of each month. Payments received by the 5th are on time. From the 6th, a late fee of €25 is added, plus €5 for each further day, up to €75 per month.

## How to pay
- Bank transfer to Northvale Rentals, IBAN IT60 X054 2811 1010 0000 0123 456. Put your flat code (for example "VR12-3B") in the payment reference.
- Standing order from your bank, set up with the same details. This is the method we recommend.
- Card payments are not accepted. Cash is not accepted at the office.

## Receipts
A receipt is emailed within 3 working days after the payment arrives. Annual statements for tax purposes are sent in January.

## Payment problems
If you cannot pay on time, email us before the 1st. We can agree on a payment plan of up to 3 months. We cannot waive late fees once they apply.

## Rent increases
Rent can change once a year, on the anniversary of your contract, in line with the ISTAT inflation index. We give at least 30 days' written notice.

## Utilities
Water and heating for the shared building are included in the monthly service charge shown on your contract. Electricity and gas inside the flat are in the tenant's name and paid directly to the supplier.
