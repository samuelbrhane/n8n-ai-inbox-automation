# Viewings, applications and moving

## Viewings
Viewings of available flats take place on weekdays between 10:00 and 18:00, and on Saturdays between 10:00 and 13:00. Each viewing takes about 20 minutes. Available times are sent by email when you ask for a viewing.

## Applying for a flat
After a viewing, you can apply by email with:
- a copy of an ID document
- proof of income (last 3 payslips, an employment contract, or a guarantor)
- your preferred move-in date

We reply to applications within 3 working days.

## Deposit
The deposit is 2 months' rent, paid before the keys are handed over. It is kept in a separate account.

## Contracts
Standard contracts are 4 years plus 4 years ("4+4"). Student contracts last 6 to 36 months. Tenants can leave early with 6 months' written notice.

## Moving in
Keys are handed over at the office after the contract is signed and the first month's rent and deposit are paid. We do a check of the flat together and record its condition with photos.

## Moving out
Give notice by email, at least 6 months before you leave (or as written in your contract). Before leaving, the flat must be clean and empty. We check the flat with you on the last day.

## Deposit return
The deposit is returned within 30 days after moving out, minus any costs for damage beyond normal wear or unpaid rent. We send an itemised list of any deductions.
