# House rules

## Quiet hours
Quiet hours are from 22:00 to 08:00 every day, and from 13:00 to 15:00 on Sundays. Music, parties and loud work should stay inside these limits.

## Noise complaints
First, if you feel comfortable, talk to your neighbour. If the problem continues, email us with the dates and times. We contact the neighbour in writing. We do not share who made the complaint.

## Pets
Cats and small dogs (up to 10 kg) are allowed with written permission. Ask by email before the pet moves in. An extra deposit of €200 applies. Other animals need our approval.

## Smoking
Smoking is not allowed inside the flats or in shared areas. Balconies are allowed if smoke does not reach other flats.

## Guests
Guests may stay up to 14 nights in a row. Longer stays or subletting need our written approval. Short-term rentals such as Airbnb are not allowed.

## Rubbish and recycling
Bins are in the courtyard. Sorting is required: paper, plastic and metal, glass, organic, and general waste. Large items like furniture must be booked with the city collection service, not left by the bins.

## Shared areas
Stairs, corridors and the courtyard must stay clear. Bikes go in the bike room, not in the stairwell.

## Changes to the flat
Painting, drilling into tiles, or changing locks needs our written permission first. Small picture nails in walls are fine.
