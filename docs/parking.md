# Parking

## Parking spaces
Some buildings have a courtyard or garage parking. A space costs €60 per month for a car and €25 per month for a motorbike, added to your rent. Spaces are assigned in order of request. Email us to join the waiting list.

## Visitors
There are no visitor spaces. Visitors must use street parking. Please check the local street signs, as some streets need a resident permit.

## Resident permits
For streets that need a resident permit, apply at the city's mobility office with your rental contract. We can send a copy of your contract by email on request.

## Rules
- Park only in your assigned space.
- Do not block the garage door, the bins or other spaces.
- Cars left unused for more than 3 months, or without valid insurance, may be removed at the owner's cost.
- Electric car charging is available only in the Via Garibaldi garage, with a separate contract.
