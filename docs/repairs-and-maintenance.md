# Repairs and maintenance

## Emergencies
An emergency is a problem that puts people or the building at risk:
- water leaking through a ceiling or wall, or a burst pipe
- no heating between November and March
- a gas smell
- no electricity in the whole flat
- a broken entrance door or window that means the flat cannot be locked

For a gas smell, leave the flat, do not use switches or flames, and call the gas emergency line on 800 900 999 first. Then tell us.

We send a technician for emergencies within 4 hours, at any time of day.

## Normal repairs
Normal repairs include a dripping tap, a broken appliance we supplied, a door that does not close properly, or mould. We arrange a visit within 5 working days. Email us with:
- your flat code and address
- what is wrong and since when
- photos if possible
- times when someone is home, or permission to enter with our key

## Who pays
We pay for repairs caused by normal wear and age. Tenants pay for damage they or their guests caused, and for blocked drains caused by things that should not go down them.

## What tenants do themselves
Replacing light bulbs, smoke alarm batteries and shower heads, and keeping drains clear.

## Boiler service
Boilers are serviced once a year between September and October. We announce the date at least 7 days in advance.
